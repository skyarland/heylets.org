PillClock

Android pill reminders on a fixed schedule, or timed from when you took the last dose.

  • Fixed times: "Lamictal at 8 PM every day."
  • Relative times: "Fiber one hour after I eat" is timed from when you actually ate.
  • Chains: each step can start the next one's clock.
  • Private: everything stays on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no internet access.
