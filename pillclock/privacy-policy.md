# PillClock privacy policy

_Last updated: November 26, 2026_

PillClock doesn't collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

- The schedule, pill names, and record of doses taken stay in the app's private storage on your device.
- The app doesn't request internet access, so it can't send data anywhere. It has no analytics, no ads, and no accounts.
- If Android's own device backup is turned on, your phone may include the app's data in your personal Google backup. That backup is managed by Google under your Google account settings, not by PillClock's developer.
- Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from the device.

Permissions and why the app uses them:

- **Notifications**: to remind you when a dose is due.
- **Alarms & reminders (exact alarms)**: so reminders fire at the scheduled minute.
- **Run at startup**: to set reminders again after the phone restarts.
- **Vibration**: for reminder notifications.

Changes: if this policy changes, the new version will be posted at https://heylets.org/privacy/ with a new date.

Contact: use the developer contact email on PillClock's Google Play listing.
